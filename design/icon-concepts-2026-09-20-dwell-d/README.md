# D-Letterform Icon Concepts — "Dwell" direction

Drawn by the coordinator 2026-09-20 as SVG, in response to the owner's direction on round 4:
keep the style of `dayflet.png` / `daysplice.png`, but make the name's initial prominent.

**These are vector sketches, not the finished identity.** The style the owner approved is a soft
dimensional render; these establish *geometry* so a render round has something settled to work from.
Codex's R55 is the request for the real thing.

## Why the round-4 mark read as an L

Structural, not a matter of degree. Dayflet's page turns from vertical to horizontal and **ends**,
so the silhouette is an L by construction — weighting it differently cannot fix that. A `D` needs a
closed or nearly-closed bowl against a vertical stem. The fold metaphor still produces one: a page
curling from the stem round to meet itself *is* a D, and the fold's underside is exactly where the
single accent plane belongs.

## The three

- **A — Folded D.** Closest to Dayflet's DNA: the curl closes into a bowl, the blue remains the
  fold's shadowed underside. The structural fix for the L problem.
- **B — Block D.** Daysplice's two offset blocks, closed into a letterform. Most calendar-like of
  the three; the seam is its weak point.
- **C — Notch D.** Notchday's metaphor — a task set into reserved time — applied to a D.

## What the size ladder says

Each concept is drawn at full size and at 120 / 80 / 40 pt. **40pt is the test**: an app icon is
read at that size on a Home Screen and in Settings, and a letterform that only resolves at 1024 has
failed. A holds its counter and still reads D. B's seam closes up and it starts to read as a solid
blob. C stays legible longest, but its notch competes with the counter for the same eye.

## Naming caveat, recorded rather than left to the render

**"Dwell" has a prominent existing brand: `dwell.com`, the architecture and interiors magazine.**
This is the same class of conflict that removed Daysplice from round 4's shortlist (Splice, in audio
and video software). No screening beyond that observation has been done here — not exhaustive, not
phonetic, no trademarks, domains or logo imagery, and no claim of legal clearance. The geometry in
these three is name-independent and carries unchanged to any other `D` name.
