## Design Review

Notchday has the most direct functional metaphor: a task fits into a reserved space in the day. Its rendering is more dimensional than ideal and can be flattened later. Dayflet keeps the folded aesthetic but can read as an L; the note-to-time-block concept needs explanation. Retain these as explorations, not approved identities. ## Dayflet

A coined blend of day and leaflet: notes and plans finding a place in your day.

Image: dayflet.png

### Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE sophisticated app-icon concept presentation for "Dayflet", a native personal productivity app combining tasks, notes and calendars. Name rationale: A coined blend of day and leaflet: notes and plans finding a place in your day. User wants simple, sleek, modern, with a tiny amount of construction detail and soft material depth. Distinctive custom silhouette and a clear functional metaphor are critical; don't imitate existing software brands. Deep charcoal rounded-square tile. Main custom symbol: a single broad warm-white vertical page-like ribbon curves downward and then bends right into a short horizontal time-block foot, making a compact asymmetric folded hook silhouette. The bend has ONE muted cornflower-blue underside visible as a narrow tapered folded surface. The negative space above the horizontal foot is a generous rectangular scheduling slot. A note physically turning into a calendar time block is the design logic. No dog-eared document outline, text lines, checkmark, clock, literal letter, book or generic S ribbon. The vertical portion is slightly left of center and the short foot reaches right; balance the whole mark centrally. TWO visible surfaces total and one fold, enough space around the symbol. Soft satin finish, minimal dimensional shading only where structural meaning requires it, crisp smooth edges. Not an illustration, not a glossy 3D toy, no floating layers, neon green, gradients as decoration, sparkles, or clutter.
